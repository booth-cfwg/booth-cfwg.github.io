About the working group

A weekly forum for presenting and discussing work in progress in corporate finance — from a first set of slides to a paper nearing submission.

The Corporate Finance Working Group brings together doctoral students, post-docs and faculty working on corporate finance and adjacent topics: capital structure, investment, corporate governance, financial intermediation, entrepreneurial finance and the boundaries between finance and macroeconomics.

The emphasis is on early-stage work. Presenters are encouraged to bring unfinished results and open questions, and the audience is expected to engage substantively. Sessions are informal: there is no discussant, and interruptions are welcome throughout.

Attending

Meetings are open to members of the University community. There is no need to register for an individual session, but joining the mailing list is the only way to receive the weekly announcement, room changes and circulated drafts.

Presenting

We welcome proposals from students at any stage. To present, email the organizers with a working title and a one-paragraph abstract; a draft is helpful but not required. Slots are typically assigned a quarter ahead, and we keep one or two open each quarter for work that comes together late.
